Privacy policy

BMI Calculator: Body Fat & BMR (Android, package bmi.calculator.bmicalculator.bodymass.bodymassindex) · Effective 14 September 2026

In short: the app does not collect, send or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone, and only leaves it when you choose to export or share it.

Who we are

The app is developed and published on Google Play by Asad Ullah Sansi ("we", "us"). For any question about privacy or this policy, email asadullahsansi@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

The app handles personal and health information that you choose to enter:

  • Details about each person: name, sex, age, height, weight, activity level, chosen comparison standard, and a calorie goal if you save one.
  • Optional body measurements (neck, waist, hip), pregnancy details, and resting heart rate, if you add them.
  • Your logged history, such as weights and measurements, and the results of calculations you run.
  • Progress photos, if you add any.
  • App settings, such as units and theme.

This information is used only to calculate and show your results inside the app. It is never used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

What we collect and share

Nothing. The app has no internet access, no account and no sign-up, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services. It cannot send your information to us or to anyone else, and no third party receives it.

The app requests no Android permissions. When you add a photo, you choose it with Android's own photo picker, and the app receives only that picture.

How your data is protected

  • Everything is kept in the app's private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps out of reach of other apps.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so your data is not copied to any backup service.
  • Photos are stored inside the app, not in your gallery.
  • On phones where device encryption is on (the default on modern Android), the stored data is encrypted along with the rest of the device.

Exporting, importing and sharing

Data leaves the app only when you ask it to:

  • Export writes a CSV or PDF file to a location you pick.
  • Import reads a backup file you pick.
  • Share hands an image of your result to the app you choose from Android's share menu.

What happens to an exported or shared file after that is up to you and the app or service you send it to.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. We never hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to keep, retrieve or delete on our side.

  • Profile → Delete my data permanently removes everything the app has stored, for every person.
  • Profile → Remove this person permanently removes one person and everything they logged.
  • History → any entry can be deleted on its own, and Clear history removes all of it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Children

The app is intended for adults. Its child and teenager mode is designed for parents checking a child's growth percentile. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not collect data from children.

Health information

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about your results.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, for example if a future version adds advertising, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
